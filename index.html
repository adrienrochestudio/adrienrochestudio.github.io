Profile

Adrien Roche

adrienroche6@gmail.com

Instagram

Paris

Adrien Roche is a French artist whose work explores accumulation, memory, material culture and the visual languages of contemporary capitalism. Through drawing, painting and mixed media, he develops compositions where industrial symbols, luxury references, popular imagery and organic forms coexist in tension. His practice draws from both classical visual culture and the aesthetics of the Anthropocene, creating works that oscillate between fascination and collapse.

Alongside his artistic practice, he has developed an international career in the cultural and creative industries, with a focus on cinema, sustainability and European cooperation. He works on international projects related to sustainable production in the film industry, cultural innovation and strategic partnerships between institutions, producers and public actors. This parallel trajectory informs his artistic work with a broader understanding of systems, infrastructures and contemporary narratives.

A graduate of Emlyon Business School after completing highly selective preparatory studies in economics and geopolitics, Adrien Roche combines artistic sensibility with strategic and international experience. He has worked between Paris, New York and multiple European countries, collaborating with galleries, film professionals, institutions and entrepreneurs. His background in business, cultural diplomacy and project development gives his work a singular positioning at the intersection of art, global culture and contemporary transformation.
