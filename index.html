Artist profile

Adrien Roche

Paris

Adrien Roche is a French multidisciplinary artist born in 2001. Raised in France while constantly moving across the world, with experiences in Miami, New York, Hong Kong, London, Berlin and across Europe, Africa and the Americas, he is now based in Paris.

His work explores presence, inheritance, accumulation, growth and existential tension. Through painting, drawing and mixed media, he develops visual compositions shaped by contradiction, between refinement and collapse, memory and projection, permanence and transformation.

Influenced by architecture, French and European painting traditions, cinema, music and contemporary visual culture, his practice constantly navigates between heritage and contemporaneity.

His background in business, sustainability, geopolitics and international cultural development gives his work a dense perspective on images, systems and contemporary transformation.
