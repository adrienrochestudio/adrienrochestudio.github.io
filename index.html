Profile

Adrien Roche

Paris

Adrien Roche is a French artist working between drawing, painting and mixed media. His work explores accumulation, memory, luxury aesthetics, industrial imagery and the visual languages of contemporary capitalism. Through recurring symbols, fragmented objects and organic forms, he develops compositions shaped by tension between refinement, collapse, nostalgia and technological culture.

Alongside his artistic practice, he has developed an international career in the cultural industries, working on projects related to cinema, sustainability and European cooperation. His background in business, geopolitics and international cultural development informs a practice situated between artistic intuition and systemic observation.

He has worked between Paris, New York and multiple European countries, collaborating with galleries, institutions, entrepreneurs and film industry professionals.
